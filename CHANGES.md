# Changes

## 1.0

The first public version.

- The SCP Wiki in a terminal: articles, series lists, hubs and the main page of 17 branches (`-b`).
- Two columns: the bookmarks and the page; back and forward as in a browser.
- Search by titles through the Crom index; on the Russian branch, full-text search too.
- Images right in the text: Sixel or `▀` characters; GIF animations.
- Hotkeys in any keyboard layout (the kitty keyboard protocol; without it, the US and Russian layouts).
- The Anubis check on scpfoundation.net.
